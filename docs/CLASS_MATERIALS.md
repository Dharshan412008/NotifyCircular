# Academic communities and study materials

Students automatically join their saved study year, department and section. For example: Second year → IT → Section A. Department and section comparisons ignore letter case and surrounding spaces. Other departments' scoped groups are hidden and cannot be joined by submitting a group ID. Whole College and unrestricted activity groups remain available.

Existing students can complete Department and Section under Account settings. Changing profile details removes old academic memberships and download access immediately. Administrators manage study year in Users. Profile details are self-reported; this feature does not verify college identity.

## Assign staff

1. Sign in as an administrator and open Groups.
2. Under Class administration, add each year, department and section needed. Classes also appear automatically when students save a complete academic profile.
3. Select the faculty accounts for each class and click Save assigned staff. Assignments are administrator-controlled and recorded in the audit history.

## Share materials

1. Faculty open Materials from the navigation (More → Materials on a phone), or Study materials in Groups.
2. Choose an assigned class and create a subject.
3. Choose the subject, enter a material title and upload a file.
4. Students open the matching section or Materials to view subjects and download files.

Any currently assigned staff member may upload to subjects in that class. Only the original uploader, while still assigned to the class, may remove their file. Removing a teaching assignment immediately removes that faculty member's upload and download access to the class. Existing class materials remain available to its students and newly assigned staff.

Files support PDF, DOCX, PNG, JPEG and WebP, up to 5 MB each, with 200 MB total storage per faculty account. File content and extension are checked. Downloads require a current session and class access. Files are saved in SQLite, so keep Railway's existing persistent data volume attached and include the database in backups. No SMTP or Google credentials are required for this feature.
